# Phase 19 decisions log

Decisions made while building [phase 19](phase-19-world-map.md) without you around. Each one says what was picked, why, and what the alternative was, so you can overrule it in review.

| #   | Decision                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   | Why                                                                                                                   | Alternative                                                                      |
| --- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1   | Only `GAME_TITLE` in `src/sim/content.ts` changes to "OverEngineered". The browser tab (`index.html` `<title>`), README, package name, repo and docs still say ISV Simulator.                                                              | You asked for no rename outside the menu.                                                                             | Also rename the browser tab title; it is one line in `index.html`.               |
| 2   | The van hops from level to level along the road; you don't drive it freely.                                                                                                                                                                | Keeps the cursor in the pure flow (the server runs it online), and a gamepad press always lands on a level.           | Free driving like Overcooked, with the cursor on the nearest flag.               |
| 3   | The map layout (level positions, road, regions, gates) is plain data in `src/flow/worldMap.ts`, not in `src/render/`.                                                                                                                      | The cursor needs positions to know which level is "left" or "up"; `flow/` must stay pure and the server needs it too. | Keep the layout in render and make the cursor walk road order only (left/right). |
| 4   | Square terrain tiles (like the office floors), not Overcooked's hexes.                                                                                                                                                                     | Fits the game's grid look and the office kit; hexes would be a second geometry system.                                | Hex tiles.                                                                       |
| 5   | Story left to right: Garage town → Startup office park → Scale-Up downtown → Enterprise city → Big Tech campus. Buildings get taller to the right.                                                                                         | Your "garage in a small town to a big office in a big city" idea, read as a road trip.                                | A winding road climbing a hill to the city.                                      |
| 6   | Menus other than the title and level select (settings, credits, lobby, profile, join code) stay DOM panels, now over the dimmed 3D map instead of a solid background.                                                                      | They are mostly text and lists; the map behind them is what makes them feel 3D.                                       | Diegetic menus in the world (signs, billboards).                                 |
| 7   | A `menuRenderer` factory option on `GameScene`, next to `roundRenderer`.                                                                                                                                                                   | Same seam as the round renderer: `GameScene` stays free of three.js and testable in Node.                             | Let the round renderer draw the map too.                                         |
| 8   | Stacked PRs, one per step, none merged; each based on the previous step's branch.                                                                                                                                                          | You review after; each PR stays small enough to read.                                                                 | One big PR.                                                                      |
| 9   | The map layout is computed from the chapter list (3 tiles per level, chapters side by side, flags zigzagging between rows 4 and 8 of a 12-deep island, a bend between each two flags), not hand-placed per level. Island is 48 × 12 tiles. | The cursor, the server and tests with made-up chapters all get a map; a new level or chapter lays itself out.         | Hand-placed coordinates per level id.                                            |
| 10  | Up/down drive to the previous or next level only when it lies that way (dot product over 0.3); left/right always walk the road order. Down on the first level does nothing.                                                                | Matches "drive the van" on a zigzag road; left/right alone can reach every level.                                     | Up/down as aliases of previous/next.                                             |
| 11  | Map camera: 56° down, 30° field of view, 24 units away; follows the selected flag left and right (held 8 tiles off the island ends) but only 15% up and down, with the flag at 66% of the screen height.                                   | The whole island depth stays in view under the card, and the view doesn't bob on the zigzag.                          | Center the camera on each flag.                                                  |
| 12  | The level card sits in a fixed spot at the top middle, not floating over the selected flag. Chapter banner top left, star total (`★ 012`) top right under the sound button, hint on a dark pill at the bottom.                             | Robust at every screen size; the flag below it has a gold ring.                                                       | A card that tracks the flag in screen space (step 3 or 6 can still do that).     |
| 13  | Flags stand beside the road on the outside of its bend; the level number lies in front of the flag, or to its left when the flag is on the far side. Ground turns from grass (Garage) to paving grey (Big Tech).                           | Keeps the road clear for the van in step 3; the number is never behind the pole.                                      | Flags on the road stops.                                                         |
| 14  | `GameScene` now also draws the stage while online (it never called the renderer's `render` there), so the map and the online rounds both redraw.                                                                                           | The online path skipped the stage render; the map needs it on online menus.                                           | —                                                                                |
