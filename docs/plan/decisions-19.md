# Phase 19 decisions log

Decisions made while building [phase 19](phase-19-world-map.md) without you around. Each one says what was picked, why, and what the alternative was, so you can overrule it in review.

| #   | Decision                                                                                                                                                                      | Why                                                                                                                   | Alternative                                                                      |
| --- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1   | Only `GAME_TITLE` in `src/sim/content.ts` changes to "OverEngineered". The browser tab (`index.html` `<title>`), README, package name, repo and docs still say ISV Simulator. | You asked for no rename outside the menu.                                                                             | Also rename the browser tab title; it is one line in `index.html`.               |
| 2   | The van hops from level to level along the road; you don't drive it freely.                                                                                                   | Keeps the cursor in the pure flow (the server runs it online), and a gamepad press always lands on a level.           | Free driving like Overcooked, with the cursor on the nearest flag.               |
| 3   | The map layout (level positions, road, regions, gates) is plain data in `src/flow/worldMap.ts`, not in `src/render/`.                                                         | The cursor needs positions to know which level is "left" or "up"; `flow/` must stay pure and the server needs it too. | Keep the layout in render and make the cursor walk road order only (left/right). |
| 4   | Square terrain tiles (like the office floors), not Overcooked's hexes.                                                                                                        | Fits the game's grid look and the office kit; hexes would be a second geometry system.                                | Hex tiles.                                                                       |
| 5   | Story left to right: Garage town → Startup office park → Scale-Up downtown → Enterprise city → Big Tech campus. Buildings get taller to the right.                            | Your "garage in a small town to a big office in a big city" idea, read as a road trip.                                | A winding road climbing a hill to the city.                                      |
| 6   | Menus other than the title and level select (settings, credits, lobby, profile, join code) stay DOM panels, now over the dimmed 3D map instead of a solid background.         | They are mostly text and lists; the map behind them is what makes them feel 3D.                                       | Diegetic menus in the world (signs, billboards).                                 |
| 7   | A `menuRenderer` factory option on `GameScene`, next to `roundRenderer`.                                                                                                      | Same seam as the round renderer: `GameScene` stays free of three.js and testable in Node.                             | Let the round renderer draw the map too.                                         |
