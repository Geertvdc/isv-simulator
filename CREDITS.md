# Credits

## Art

- **Tile and wall sprites** (`public/assets/tiles/`, `public/assets/walls/`, `public/assets/floors/`): generated with [PixelLab](https://pixellab.ai) (Pro Flash and Pixen edit), from our placeholder blocks as reference. Each wall theme is cut from one generated wall strip; the wall decorations and floor tiles are generated with Pixen. Use is subject to the [PixelLab Terms of Service](https://pixellab.ai/termsofservice).
